# Three calm invoice follow-up messages

Use these as starting points only. Check the invoice, agreed payment terms and local rules before sending. Replace every bracketed field. Do not add late fees unless they were agreed in the contract and are lawful where you and the client operate.

## 1. Due-date reminder

**Subject:** Invoice [invoice number] — due [due date]

Hi [client name],

A quick note that invoice [invoice number] for [amount] is due on [due date]. I've attached it again here for convenience.

If payment is already arranged, thank you — please ignore this reminder.

Best,  
[your name]

## 2. First overdue reminder

**Subject:** Follow-up: invoice [invoice number]

Hi [client name],

I’m following up on invoice [invoice number] for [amount], which was due on [due date]. Could you let me know when payment is expected, or tell me if you need the invoice resent?

Thanks,  
[your name]

## 3. Payment-date confirmation

**Subject:** Payment date for invoice [invoice number]

Hi [client name],

I’m checking in again about invoice [invoice number] for [amount]. Could you confirm the date the payment is scheduled?

If there is an issue with the invoice or timing, please let me know so we can agree on the next step.

Best,  
[your name]

---

**Safety note:** Keep communication factual and courteous. Pause further work only where your agreement permits it. This template is not legal, tax or debt-collection advice.
