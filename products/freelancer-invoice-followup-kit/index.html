Freelancer Invoice Follow-up Kit

A simple, relationship-safe way to keep track of unpaid invoices and send clear reminders without rewriting the same message every time.

Free research preview. This is not a paid product yet. No checkout is enabled, no customer data is collected, and no email is sent automatically. The sample files are free to use.

1. A simple invoice tracker

Record the invoice, due date, amount paid, balance, status and next follow-up date in a spreadsheet-friendly CSV.

2. Three polite reminders

Use a friendly due-date note, a short overdue reminder and a clear follow-up that asks for a payment date.

3. Keep control

You decide whether and when to send each message. The preview does not connect to email, banks or client systems.

Who this is for

Solo freelancers, consultants, designers, editors and small service businesses that invoice clients directly and want a consistent, courteous follow-up process.

What this is not

  • Not a debt-collection service or legal advice.
  • Not an accounting system or payment processor.
  • Not an automatic email sender. You review and send every message yourself.
  • It does not calculate late fees or make claims about local payment laws.
Early validation asset. No paid offer, customer, sale or revenue is claimed. The full workbook and any optional automation will only be developed after evidence of buyer demand and a verified, eligible payment route.
