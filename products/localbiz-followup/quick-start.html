Stop losing customer enquiries in chat threads

LocalBiz FollowUp is a lightweight browser-based desk for recording enquiries, tracking the next action and preparing a polite follow-up message. This guide shows a realistic workflow for a small online shop.

Example: a customer asks about a product

A customer sends an Instagram message asking whether a particular item is available and how much delivery costs. You reply with the details, but the customer does not decide immediately. Without a simple record, the enquiry can disappear under newer messages.

  1. Open LocalBiz FollowUp in your browser.
  2. Add the customer's name and the contact method they used. Record only the minimum information you need.
  3. Enter the product or service they asked about.
  4. Set a sensible next follow-up date and choose the current stage, such as New or Waiting.
  5. When the date arrives, filter for due enquiries and select Draft to prepare a message.
  6. Review the message, edit it to fit the conversation, then copy it or open the WhatsApp draft. You decide whether and when to send it.
  7. Update the stage when the customer buys, declines or needs more time.

Example follow-up message

Hi Sara, I’m following up on your question about the blue backpack. Would you like me to confirm the available colours or delivery options for you?

This is a starting point, not an automatic message. Personalize it and make sure the customer has agreed to be contacted through that channel.

A simple daily routine

  • Start of day: open the “Due today / overdue” filter.
  • Before contacting anyone: check the conversation history and respect opt-outs or requests not to be contacted.
  • After each reply: update the stage and choose the next follow-up date only if another contact is appropriate.
  • End of day: export a CSV or JSON backup if you need an external record.

Privacy and product limits

  • Records are stored in the current browser's local storage; they are not automatically synced between devices.
  • Use the export buttons for backups. Clearing browser data can remove records.
  • Do not store payment-card details, passwords, identity documents or sensitive personal information.
  • The app prepares message drafts; it does not send WhatsApp or Instagram messages automatically.
  • There is no WhatsApp API, cloud account, background reminder service or built-in payment processing in this MVP.
