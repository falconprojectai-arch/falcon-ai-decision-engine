Best low-friction candidates

1. AgentMart — reusable downloads

Best fit for a small, ready-made skill or workflow pack. Sellers keep 97% after the 3% platform fee; checkout supports USDC on Base. Uploads are scanned before publishing. The owner must verify the store through an email link, and a real Base payout address must be configured for x402 checkout.

Official docs: seller and API guide · terms.

2. Colony HQ — prompt-driven agents

Potentially the shortest route to a paid agent listing: the publish page exposes a form for name, description, category, model, system prompt and price. The platform states that creators receive 80% in USDC on Base and do not need an account or KYC. However, the public home page showed no published agents, users or creator payouts at review time, so treat it as an early experiment rather than proven demand.

Publish page: Colony publish form.

3. Soul.Markets — per-job expertise

Suitable when the product is a repeatable service such as research, analysis or workflow planning. Sellers retain 80% and the published payout guide sets a $10 minimum. A wallet on Base can be linked for payouts. The service must actually be deliverable; a static prompt alone is not proof of a working service.

Official docs: introduction · seller guide.

What is prepared

A privacy-safe Lead Follow-up Strategist package has been added to the public source repository. It turns a genuine inbound enquiry into a suggested next action, a short message draft, a conservative follow-up schedule and CRM fields. It does not send messages, scrape contacts, place calls or claim bookings.

  • Open the skill package and test cases
  • Designed for local service businesses, shops and online sellers.
  • Includes consent checks, opt-out handling, privacy minimization and five acceptance tests.

Important safeguards

  • Do not publish customer records, contact lists, credentials, private prompts or confidential business details.
  • Do not promise that a skill will send messages or book customers unless those integrations exist and are authorized.
  • Never put a wallet private key, seed phrase, API key or store secret in a public repository or chat.
  • Do not pay for boosts, deposits or paid tiers just to test a marketplace. Prefer free listing routes.
  • Count only verified on-chain settlement as revenue. Listings, views, installs and pending balances are not income.

Program terms and product availability are controlled by each provider. This comparison is informational and may become outdated.
