## Distinct opportunity investigation: AI-agent task marketplaces
### Claw Earn (USDC on Base)
- Public marketplace page reported **0 available tasks** at inspection; it showed 82 completed tasks. This is a snapshot, not a stable supply statistic.
- Payment is USDC on Base; approved delivery pays 90% to worker and 10% platform fee.
- Human-flow tasks have a 9 USDC minimum. First task stake is 30% of task value; subsequent trust tiers reduce it. Base gas is also needed.
- Wallet-only participation is described, but the task stake, gas, and lack of open tasks mean this is **not zero-upfront-cost** and is not currently a Golden River candidate.
- Sources: https://aiagentstore.ai/claw-earn · https://aiagentstore.ai/claw-earn/ai-agent-tasks/available

### AI Agent Store jobs
- Platform advertises 15% seller fee (minimum $1); payouts use Stripe in supported countries or USDC through Claw Earn.
- This is task work, not passive recurring income; human review/delivery and payout setup remain necessary.
- Source: https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-jobs

### Mercatai
- B2B-only, requires the operator to be a legal entity/business; Stripe Connect identity verification is required before payment/payout. Payout is to a bank account, not crypto.
- 0% marketplace fee on first 10 tasks but a 0.8% processing deduction remains; then 4.2% marketplace fee plus that deduction. Stripe fees may also apply.
- Not suitable for the current crypto-only payout constraint unless the user's circumstances and eligibility materially change.
- Sources: https://www.mercatai.eu/terms · https://www.mercatai.eu/ai-agents

### Digital-product storefronts
- Ko-fi states no listing/monthly fee and a 5% shop fee, but payments go directly to PayPal or Stripe.
- Payhip states a free plan with 5% transaction fee, but customer payment methods are PayPal/cards through payment processors. A free storefront does not solve payout eligibility or buyer acquisition.
- Not selected until a usable payment processor is confirmed.
- Sources: https://ko-fi.com/shop · https://payhip.com/pricing
