## 3. Separate deep-web research — independent of Falcon

Six materially different angles were compared: AI-agent-run services, local-business workflow automation, digital products, niche data/research, affiliate/lead-routing, and food-business cost control.

### Evidence and comparison
1. **AI-augmented services:** Upwork's 2026 Future Workforce Index reports AI work earning a 34% hourly premium, while AI-augmented professional services grew 72% and earnings rose 22%; low-complexity generative execution grew in contract starts but per-contract earnings fell. This supports expert workflow outcomes more than generic AI output. Source: https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026
2. **Local-business follow-up automation:** Qpost Smile advertises a $0 forever tier and $18/month Pro; Nudgelet lists $29/$79/$149 monthly plans with a 14-day trial. Demand exists, but the category is crowded and basic feedback/follow-up features already have free or trial-based competitors. Sources: https://qpost.com/smile · https://nudgelet.com/pricing
3. **Digital products:** Etsy's official seller-country list excludes Iran, and Etsy Payments requires a valid bank account in an eligible country. Etsy also prohibits AI-generated functional instructions; seller-designed digital items must meet its creativity rules. Global Etsy is not a viable assumed payout route for an Iran-based seller. Sources: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015710408-Countries-Eligible-for-Etsy-Payments · https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360024112614-What-Can-I-Sell-on-Etsy
4. **Niche data/research products:** compiling live ingredient or supplier price datasets would create ongoing refresh, source-licensing and accuracy work. No sufficiently strong zero-cost data licensing path was established in this run.
5. **Affiliate/lead-routing:** Amazon Associates requires a qualifying owned “Site” and properly tagged Special Links; it is audience-dependent and does not solve the current Iran payout constraint. Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement · https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
6. **Food-business costing:** DishCost charges $39/month but offers free calculators; MarketMan is listed at $249/month and MarginEdge at $350/location/month. CostMyDish has a free plan for up to 5 dishes and 20 ingredients, with a $4.99/month upgrade; DashDinner and SnagATool publish free spreadsheet templates. This proves a recognized category and strong competition, not demand for our prototype. No strong Persian-language demand signal was verified in this search. Sources: https://dishcost.com/pricing · https://www.costmydish.com/ · https://dashdinner.com/tools/food-cost-spreadsheet · https://snagatool.com/templates/recipe-cost-calculator

### Selected test hypothesis
**Street Food Unit Economics Kit** — an offline spreadsheet for small street-food sellers, food trucks, sandwich kiosks and home caterers. Differentiation hypothesis: simpler local-currency costing for batch yield, packaging, labor, energy, waste and break-even, without a cloud account or subscription.

**Concrete advancement completed:** created a six-sheet XLSX prototype and README locally; formulas were recalculated with LibreOffice using a synthetic QA scenario. Test outputs: sample unit cost 57,765 toman; suggested price 165,043 toman at a 65% target gross margin; with 300,000 toman sample daily fixed cost, break-even was 2.67 units; 20 synthetic sales at 170,000 toman generated 3,400,000 toman revenue and 1,886,934 toman estimated operating result before tax. These are synthetic test values, not actual costs, sales, forecasts or customer evidence.

**Status:** `researched-not-submitted`; local prototype only. No public listing, buyer test, account or payment setup. Product file is not uploaded to the repository.  
**Next validation:** test the workbook with three opt-in small food vendors; proceed only if they can enter a real recipe and correctly interpret unit cost, suggested price and break-even without assisted setup. Do not post unsolicited messages.
