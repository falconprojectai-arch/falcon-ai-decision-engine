## 6. Separate market-angle comparison

1. **AI-agent-run services / AI receptionist:** Zoom lists $29.99 for 100 minutes; Greet lists $49.95–$199.95/month; Belline lists $69–$279/month. This is real paid competition, but phone-minute costs, number provisioning, business-specific onboarding, call recording/consent, escalation design and support make a zero-upfront, no-routine-labor launch weak. Sources: https://zoom.us/pricing/receptionist and https://usegreet.com/
2. **Local-business workflow automation:** GT-Car's 499k–999k toman monthly pricing shows a paying-category hypothesis, but building a full SaaS and supporting each shop is too heavy for the current constraints. The ECU form pack is a narrower complementary asset.
3. **Digital products/content:** The inspected pet-sitting forms bundle is listed at $12, has two item reviews, and its seller's shop shows 5.7K overall sales. A second listing from another shop had no item reviews despite 1.8K overall shop sales. Etsy provides instant downloads, but charges $0.20 per listing and 6.5% transaction fees; Etsy Payments requires a bank account in an eligible country. No Iran payout route was verified, so Etsy is not the selected route. Sources: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1681785189/pet-sitting-forms-bundle-pet-sitting and https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy and https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002120927-What-is-Etsy-Payments
4. **Niche data/research products:** automotive wiring/rate-sheet packages attract attention, but compiling and updating technical data has copyright, accuracy and maintenance risks. The current forms prototype avoids copying technical datasets.
5. **Marketplaces / affiliate / lead-routing:** Digyno offers automatic delivery and a 3% fee, but requires eKYC and store approval; payout is held for 72 hours after delivery or until buyer confirmation. No affiliate/lead-routing program with a verified zero-cost payout path was found in this pass.
