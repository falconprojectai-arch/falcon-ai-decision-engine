## 6. Iran / Persian / rial monetization audit

### Digyno — strongest current fit for a downloadable file
Official seller page: https://digyno.com/sell
- No registration or monthly fee; 3% commission per sale.
- Automatic file delivery immediately after successful payment.
- New seller funds are held for 72 hours until 5 successful sales; then the usual hold is 24 hours. A buyer confirmation can release earlier.
- Minimum withdrawal: 100,000 toman; payout is usually within one business day after withdrawal request.
- Seller onboarding requires an Iranian mobile number in the seller's own name, electronic identity verification (ID/date-of-birth match, documents and selfie video), and administrator approval.
- Payout Sheba must be in the seller's own name and match the verified identity.
- XLSX, PDF, ZIP, DOCX and CSV are accepted, up to 100 MB per file; macros/scripts/executables are prohibited.
- Refund complaints are held and reviewed by support before release.
- This is a marketplace/merchant platform, not a guarantee of traffic or sales. No account was created and no KYC was attempted.

The setup guide also requires registered site/domain, tax-file Sheba, Iran-based server IP and an electronic contract. ### International rails and sanctions caveat
- Etsy Payments' official eligible-country list does not include Iran: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015710408-Countries-Eligible-for-Etsy-Payments.
- Payhip's free plan is $0/month + 5%, but customer payments are through PayPal or cards via Stripe; these payout rails are not verified for this seller: https://payhip.com/pricing.

- This is a practical eligibility screen, not legal advice. Provider rules and sanctions obligations must be checked before any cross-border transaction.
