## 6. Iran / Persian / rial payment rails

Official provider pages were checked on 2026-11-06:

- **ZarinPal:** [gateway](https://www.zarinpal.com/payment-gateway) requires registration, identity verification, application and document review; settlement is daily, usually the next day by 17:00. [Pricing](https://www.zarinpal.com/pricing) states first month free except Shaparak fees, then 0.5% capped at 16,000 toman + 500 toman fixed; eNAMAD issuance is listed at 600,000 toman.
- **PayPing:** [pricing](https://payping.ir/pricing/) lists a base fee of 1% up to 5,000 toman + 500 toman fixed + VAT; product/invoice links, web services, refunds and automatic daily settlement are listed. [FAQ](https://payping.ir/faq/) requires identity, bank and business details, an active tax file/tax tracking code and eNAMAD; payments before 22:00 usually settle in the second cycle the following day.
- **Zibal:** [registration guide](https://help.zibal.ir/article/registration/) requires an Iranian mobile number in the applicant's name, email, same-name IBAN and original identity document; tax tracking code, valid eNAMAD and a valid permit are listed as prerequisites. [Pricing](https://zibal.ir/pricing) lists 1% capped at 20,000 toman, plus 10% VAT on the fee; no setup or monthly fee; settlement is periodic and account-configured, with faster settlement configurable in the panel.
- **Digyno:** [seller terms](https://digyno.com/sell) advertise no registration/monthly fee, 3% per sale, automatic file delivery, 100,000-toman minimum withdrawal, same-name Shaba and eKYC/store/product approval. New sellers have a 72-hour hold until five successful sales, then 24 hours; refunds/disputes are handled during the review period. XLSX/PDF/DOCX are accepted; macro-enabled or executable files are not.
- **IDPay:** official pages timed out/returned 502 during this run; current fees, onboarding and settlement are not verified.
- **Etsy:** its [official eligible-country list](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015710408-Countries-Eligible-for-Etsy-Payments) does not include Iran and requires the shop's bank country to match the bank account.
- **Manual card-to-card:** no official bank/provider rule and reconciliation flow was verified; it is not recommended as an automated checkout.
- **Sanctions scope:** [OFAC FAQ 1250](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1250), dated 2026-05-01, addresses Iranian digital-asset exchanges and U.S. persons/jurisdiction. It is not a blanket statement about every domestic rial payment; cross-border activity requires case-specific legal review.
