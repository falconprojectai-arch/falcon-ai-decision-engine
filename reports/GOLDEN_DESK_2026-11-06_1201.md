## 7. Iran / Persian / rial payment rails

- **ZarinPal official:** identity verification and document review are required. Its public page states daily/weekly/monthly settlement options, first month free except Shaparak fees, then 0.5% capped at 16,000 toman + 500 toman fixed. The API uses a merchant ID, payment request, callback, and verification flow. https://www.zarinpal.com/payment-gateway · https://www.zarinpal.com/docs/paymentGateway/connectToGateway
- **PayPing official:** base plan is 1% capped at 5,000 toman + 500 fixed + VAT; automatic daily Shaparak settlement; merchant onboarding requires identity, bank and business details, an active tax file/tax tracking code, and eNAMAD for the online gateway. It supports payment/invoice links, a hosted store, refunds, and API services. https://payping.ir/pricing/ · https://payping.ir/faq/ · https://docs.payping.ir/
- **Zibal official:** 1% per successful transaction capped at 20,000 toman, plus 10% VAT on the fee; no setup or monthly fee; settlement is account-cycle based. Individual onboarding requires an Iranian mobile in the applicant's name, same-name Shaba, identity document, tax tracking code, valid eNAMAD, and valid permit. https://zibal.ir/pricing · https://help.zibal.ir/article/registration/
- **Digyno official:** no registration/monthly fee, 3% per sale, automatic file delivery, eKYC, Iranian mobile in seller's name, same-name Shaba, store and product approval, 100,000 toman minimum withdrawal; new sellers have a 72-hour hold for the first five successful sales, then 24 hours. PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV and other static formats are accepted; executable scripts/macros are not. https://digyno.com/sell
- **IDPay:** official docs/fees/KYC/settlement were not verified in this run. https://idpay.ir/web-service/v1.1/
- Manual card-to-card was not selected: no auditable merchant reconciliation and refund process was verified.
- The current receiving constraint remains crypto-only, so none of these rial rails is yet confirmed usable for this operator. No identity, card, IBAN, account, or payout details were requested, stored, or transmitted.
- **Sanctions caution:** OFAC FAQ 1250 (2026-05-01) says Iranian digital-asset exchanges meet the definition of Iranian financial institutions and their property within U.S. jurisdiction or control of U.S. persons is blocked. This is not a blanket statement about domestic rial gateways. The operator's legal nexus is not established here; cross-border/crypto eligibility requires qualified legal review before execution. https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1250
