## Product listings

### LocalBiz FollowUp
- URL: https://falconprojectai-arch.github.io/falcon-ai-decision-engine/products/localbiz-followup/
- Category: Business / CRM / Productivity
- Tagline: Simple customer follow-up for small businesses.
- Description: Track customer requests, sales status, next follow-up dates, and draft replies in one lightweight workspace. Export your records to CSV. Designed for small shops and online sellers that need a simple follow-up routine without a complex CRM.
- Tags: CRM, customer follow-up, small business, sales, CSV, productivity

### StockPulse
- URL: https://falconprojectai-arch.github.io/falcon-ai-decision-engine/products/stockpulse/
- Category: Business / Inventory
- Tagline: Lightweight inventory and reorder tracking for small shops.
- Description: Keep a simple product list, track stock levels, flag low inventory, and estimate inventory value and gross margin. Import and export CSV files to work with existing spreadsheets.
- Tags: inventory, stock management, reorder alerts, retail, CSV, small business

### MCPGuard Lite
- URL: https://falconprojectai-arch.github.io/falcon-ai-decision-engine/products/mcpguard-lite/
- Category: Developer Tools / Security / AI
- Tagline: Quick local checks for risky MCP and AI-agent configuration patterns.
- Description: Inspect agent and MCP configuration files for common risk indicators such as exposed-looking secrets, excessive permissions, unsafe command execution, and insecure connection patterns. This is a lightweight heuristic checker, not a complete security audit or runtime protection system.
- Tags: MCP, AI agents, developer tools, configuration security, local scanner

### ExpenseSnap
- URL: https://falconprojectai-arch.github.io/falcon-ai-decision-engine/products/expensesnap/
- Category: Productivity / Finance
- Tagline: Track project hours and expenses in one simple workspace.
- Description: Record billable work time and project expenses, calculate totals, and prepare a clean summary for invoicing. A lightweight option for freelancers who do not need a full accounting suite.
- Tags: time tracking, expenses, freelancer, project billing, productivity

### CronPulse
- URL: https://falconprojectai-arch.github.io/falcon-ai-decision-engine/products/cronpulse/
- Category: Developer Tools / DevOps
- Tagline: Find recurring failures in scheduled-job and agent run logs.
- Description: Import execution history from cron jobs or automated agents, identify repeated failures, and produce a concise diagnostic summary. It analyzes supplied logs offline; it does not provide live monitoring or alert delivery.
- Tags: cron, job logs, DevOps, automation, AI agents, troubleshooting

## Outreach rules

- Prefer answering a relevant public question with a useful example over unsolicited direct messages.
- Do not scrape personal contact details or mass-message users.
- Never claim users, installs, customers, security certification, uptime monitoring, or revenue without evidence.
- Do not pay for listings or create accounts using unverified identity details.
- Record each submission as: drafted / submitted / pending review / approved / rejected / live.
- Measure referral visits, product interactions, feedback, and paid conversions separately.
