# Lead Follow-up Strategist

A compact, reusable agent skill for small local businesses. It turns an inbound enquiry into a consent-aware next action, message draft, conservative follow-up schedule and CRM update checklist.

## Package contents
- `SKILL.md`: operating instructions and safety boundaries
- `input-schema.json`: structured input contract
- `TEST_CASES.md`: five acceptance tests

## Who it is for
Local service businesses, independent shops and online sellers handling inbound enquiries and quote requests.

## What it does not do
It does not send messages, scrape contacts, place calls, book customers, take payments or make legal compliance determinations. It drafts; the business approves and sends.

## Suggested marketplace positioning
- Short description: "Turn inbound customer enquiries into respectful, consent-aware follow-up drafts, next steps and CRM updates."
- Suggested introductory price: $1.99 per download or low per-use price if the marketplace supports metered execution.
- Suggested categories: sales, customer support, local business, lead management.
